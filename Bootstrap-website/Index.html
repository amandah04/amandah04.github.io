Welcome to My Digital Water Journal!

I built this journal to document my water adventures, capture my stories, and share the moments that shaped my life on the ocean. The sea hooked me early and beach trips, boat rides, and SCUBA diving ignited a passion that never faded. At 10, I earned my Virginia Boater's License. By 11, I captained my own trips, steering my sisters through the inlets of my grandmother's bay and exploring every corner of the water I could reach. Captivated by marine life, I pursued a Biology degree from Virginia Tech and will continue to study and protect our ocean ecosystems.
