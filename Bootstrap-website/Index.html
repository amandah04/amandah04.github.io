Welcome to My Digital Water Journal!

This site is a collection of my water related adventures, stories, pictures, and struggles. My love for the ocean came at a very young age when me and my family would go out to the beach, go on boats, and when I learned how to SCUBA dive. At the age of 10, I got my Virginia Boaters license, and at 11 I was taking myself and my sisters out to explore inlets on the bay my grandmother lived on.
