Live-Aboard Adventure

5 Days on the Morning Star

Blackbeard's Cruises · The Bahamas

Five days aboard the Morning Star with Blackbeard's Cruises — one of the most legendary live-aboard dive operations in the Bahamas. We ate, slept, and breathed diving from sunup to sundown.

Highlights

  • Dove a blue hole — eerie, dark, and unforgettable
  • Fed Caribbean reef sharks on a guided shark dive
  • Dropped off a sheer wall dive into open blue water
  • Rode a drift current through a canyon passage
  • Explored several shallow reefs teeming with color and life
