My SCUBA Adventures

Certifications

PADI Certified Diver

Open Water

I learned to master buoyancy, gear, and the basics of exploring the underwater world down to 60ft. Open water started with understanding how to deal with basic emergencies, dive planning, and underwater communication.

Advanced Open Water

I learned the risks of deep dives, navigation, night diving, and planning dives down to 100ft. This allowed me to explore new environments and push my comfort zone.

Rescue Diver

The most challenging and rewarding certificaiton, learning to manage emergencies, assist distressed divers, and lead with calm under pressure.

Live-Aboard Adventure

5 Days on the Morning Star

Blackbeard's Cruises · The Bahamas

Five days aboard the Morning Star with Blackbeard's Cruises, one of the most legendary live-aboard dive operations in the Bahamas. We ate, slept, and breathed diving from sunup to sundown. The boat made approximately 5 dive stops a day.

Highlights

  • Dove a blue hole which went to almost 300 feet, the size and depth of this feature were unforgettable and the 2nd year we experienced a shark mating event that included 100s of sharks
  • Fed Caribbean reef sharks on a guided shark dive
  • Visited a continental shelf drop off where I experienced a giant spotted eagle manta ray making his way through the water
  • Rode a drift current through a reef passage, it felt like a lazy river but for divers
  • Explored several shallow reefs teeming with bright corals, parrot fish, nudibranchs, and other marine life
