My Story

Growing up going to Long Island, New York, every summer the water always a part of my life. My grandmother lived on a bay, and some of my earliest memories involve watching the tide come in, digging for clams, and exploring inlets from a tiny fiberglass boat.

By age 10, I'd earned my Virginia Boater's License, the youngest you can in the state of Virginia. A year later, I was the one driving and taking my younger sisters out to explore.

Before high school, I went the local sailing club and fell in love with racing and exploring. I raced small boats across the Great Peconic bay, learning to read the water and the weather. It was a different way to connect with the bay, but it only deepened my love for it.

This journal documents the dives, the trips, the weird creatures, and every place the water has carried me. Im hoping to inspire others to get out there.

My Journey

Age 10 — Virginia Boater's License

Growing up on sailboats and motorboats, my family encouraged me to get licensed as soon as possible.

Age 11 — Bay Exploration and Boating Trips

Started captaining an aluminum skiff on Grandma's bay. Sisters in tow. I eventually worked my way up to driving our family's motorboat.

Age 12 — Watersports

I participated in various watersports activities, including wakesurfing and water skiing.

Age 13- PADI Certified

Earned open-water SCUBA certification and learned in a quarry in Virginia called lake pheonix. My first real ocean dive: Bahamas live-aboard.

Virginia Tech — Biology

I got the privilege to study ocean invertebrates, fresh and saltwater ecology, and some marine microbiology.
