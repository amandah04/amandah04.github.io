My Story

Growing up going to Long Island, New York, every summer the water always a part of my life. My grandmother lived on a bay, and some of my earliest memories involve watching the tide come in, digging for clams, and exploring inlets from a tiny fiberglass boat.

By age 10, I'd earned my Virginia Boater's License, the youngest you can in the state of Virginia. A year later, I was the one driving and taking my younger sisters out to explore. The exploring different shores and waters wasn't something that scared me. It felt like home.

Before high school, I went the local sailing club and fell in love with racing and exploring. There's something uniquely satisfying about harnessing the wind. I raced Sunfish and 420s across the Great Peconic bay, learning to read the water and the weather. It was a different way to connect with the bay, but it only deepened my love for it.

This journal is my way of keeping track of it all — the dives, the trips, the weird creatures, the places the water has taken me. I hope it inspires you to get out there too.

My Journey

Age 8 — First Beach Scuba Intro

Tried a discover-scuba session on vacation. Was immediately obsessed.

Age 10 — Virginia Boater's License

One of the youngest licensed boaters in the class.

Age 11 — Bay Exploration Trips

Started captaining an aluminum skiff on Grandma's bay. Sisters in tow.

Age 12/13- Watersports

I started participating in various watersports activities, including wakesurfing and water skiing.

High School — PADI Certified

Earned open-water SCUBA certification. First real ocean dive: Bahamas live-aboard.

Virginia Tech — Biology

I got the privilege to study ocean invertebrates, fresh and saltwater ecology, and some marine microbiology.

What I'm Into

SCUBA Diving

Open water, reef dives, night dives — every dive teaches me something new about what lives below the surface.

Sailing

Racing, cruising, or just drifting — there's nothing like a stiff breeze and a close-hauled tack.

Marine Invertebrates

Horseshoe crabs, jellyfish, nudibranchs — I'm fascinated by the invertebrate world and its role in the ecosystem.

Underwater Photography

Learning to capture the beauty of reef ecosystems with a waterproof camera rig.

Kayaking & Paddleboarding

When the waves are calm, I love exploring coastal marshes and estuaries up close by paddle.

Ocean Storytelling

Writing about my adventures and the science of the sea to share the ocean with people who haven't seen it.
