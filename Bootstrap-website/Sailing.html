Sailing the Chesapeake

My favorite memories were those aboard my mom's 62-foot Catalina named Hard Labor on the Chesapeake Bay. My family spent many weekends on the chesapeake together and I still remeber the first time my mom left me at the helm to take a nap below.

We spent many weekends traveling to a small island called Tangier where we ate a lot of crab and explored the 1.2 square mile island with a population of approximately 400 people. For years feral cats roamed the island living off crab and bugs. One day a small black cat wandered onto the boat and thats how we got our first cat, Tangier. Needless to say, sailing was a big part of our family life and I will always charish those memories.
