On the Water

Sailing the Chesapeake

Some of my favorite memories on the water happened aboard my mom's 62-foot Catalina on the Chesapeake Bay. There's something humbling about handling a boat that size every tack, every gust, every shift in the wind demands your full attention.

On one particularly magical afternoon, a pod of sailfish found us. They darted alongside the hull, disappeared beneath the bow, and surfaced again off the stern playing tag with us for nearly an hour. It's the kind of moment that reminds you the bay is alive, and you're just a guest in it.
